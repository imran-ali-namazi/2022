IDEAS is the "Institute for Development of Effective Applications of Spirituality", a new age look at rationalism, religion and spirituality with a focus on synthesizing them and buildng a one world philosophy.

It believes, as the [Universal Life Church](https://ulc.org/) does, that we are all children of the same universe.

> Religion is a great force - the only real motive force in the world; but what you fellows don't understand is that you must get at a man through his own religion and not through yours. <br />George Bernard Shaw

I believe that there is a balance between religion and science, spirituality and atheism. God and Man. In 2015, I spent 4 hours choosing the best quotes from wikiquote and made this page on [religion](https://legacy.yieldmore.org/topics/religion/). I also, before I started writing poetry, made [this list of 128 attributes of the divine](https://imran.yieldmore.org/divine/). I then spent a few years wrestling with the [problem of religion](https://imran.yieldmore.org/imagine/), then turned to writing poeems on [spirit](https://imran.yieldmore.org/poems/category/spirit/), a [one world philosophy](https://imran.yieldmore.org/poems/category/imagine/), more dedicated to the work of [Mother Sri Aurobindo](https://imran.yieldmore.org/impelled/for/msa/), some [devotional](https://imran.yieldmore.org/poems/category/devotional/) and finally a series of prose on [evolution and the new age of illumination](https://imran.yieldmore.org/poems/category/devotional/).
