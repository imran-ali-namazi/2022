# Scope of 5D Center

* 5D is a potential bluprint to bring in a stress free and progressive work culture.
* 5D develops "shared vision" and inculcates sense of ownership
* 5D enhances employee zeal to optimize potential towards organizational goal.
* 5D builds a practical context for a collective willingness to change.
* 5D helps the management to gain a precise insight: to redefine policies,improve systems and processes in the organization.
