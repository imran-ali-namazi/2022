A Crowdsourced Learning Platform in 5 or 10 levels using [Amadeus](../tech-and-web/), conceived in Jan 2022.

Some programs / courses that we thought of are:

* Childhood
* Educating Youth
* Higher and Technical Education
* Living
* Integral Healing
* Life Skills
* Raising Humanity
* Parenting

Each could have sections like:

* degress
* exercises
* notes
* submissions

and can completely be authored in makdown, google drive, html presentations and SCORM
